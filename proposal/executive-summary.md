# Executive Summary

**Submitted to:** J. Okafor, Director of Procurement, Meridian Components, Inc.
**RFP Reference:** MC-2026-0417
**Response Date:** April 27, 2026

---

Meridian Components has built a functional inventory dashboard — but an incomplete delivery from the previous vendor has left the operations team working around known defects, and IT unable to approve changes without test coverage. The result is a system your team depends on daily that cannot safely evolve.

We propose to close that gap in a focused, fixed-fee engagement: remediate the Reports module, build the Restocking recommendations view your operations team has requested, establish automated browser test coverage to satisfy IT's requirements, and deliver architecture documentation suitable for long-term internal ownership.

Our approach is grounded in the actual codebase. We reviewed the previous vendor's handoff materials and the source code as part of preparing this response. The existing Vue 3 / FastAPI stack is sound; the issues are in the application layer, not the foundation. That means faster delivery and lower risk than a rebuild.

We understand the budget is constrained. Our proposal is structured around the four required deliverables (R1–R4) at a fixed fee, with the three desired items (D1–D3) scoped as optional additions. You will not be asked to choose between quality and completeness on the required work.

**What we are committing to:**
- Full remediation of all logged and discovered Reports defects (R1)
- A working Restocking view with budget ceiling and purchase order recommendations (R2)
- End-to-end browser test coverage across all four critical flows (R3)
- Current-state architecture documentation ready for IT handoff (R4)
- Delivery in eight weeks from contract execution

Meridian's operations team should be able to trust their tools. We're here to make that happen.
